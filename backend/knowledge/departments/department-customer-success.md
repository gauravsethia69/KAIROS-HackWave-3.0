# Customer Success operating reference

## Status and primary responsibilities
This is a proposed operating description for Customer Success, not a claim that the department has accepted new work. The proposed remit is client outcomes, meeting preparation and adoption follow-through.

## Relevant workflow
Prepare a client brief from the account manager link, upcoming meetings, previous commitments and unresolved issues. Start with source IDs and current ownership; distinguish completed work from open obligations.

## Records and relationships
Relevant records include client accounts, meetings, communications and enablement tasks. Actual employees are selected by employees.department_id. Their tasks and issues follow owner_employee_id; project ownership is a separate relationship and must not be inferred from task counts.

## Dependencies
Coordinate with Support for active incidents; Sales for contract interpretation; Data & Analytics for adoption measurements. Shared client_id, product_id and project_id relationships provide context, not implicit reassignment.

## Escalation
Propose contacting the assigned employee first, then their recorded manager if available. State missing owners or schedules explicitly. Only an approved company workflow may change an assignment or commitment.
