# Sales operating reference

## Status and primary responsibilities
This is a proposed operating description for Sales, not a claim that the department has accepted new work. The proposed remit is commercial coordination and contract-owner engagement.

## Relevant workflow
Check recorded status and renewal dates; do not infer legal obligations or promise pricing changes from this workspace. Start with source IDs and current ownership; distinguish completed work from open obligations.

## Records and relationships
Relevant records include contracts, client-product relationships and commercial communications. Actual employees are selected by employees.department_id. Their tasks and issues follow owner_employee_id; project ownership is a separate relationship and must not be inferred from task counts.

## Dependencies
Coordinate with Customer Success for account context; Operations for delivery feasibility; recorded contract owner for terms. Shared client_id, product_id and project_id relationships provide context, not implicit reassignment.

## Escalation
Propose contacting the assigned employee first, then their recorded manager if available. State missing owners or schedules explicitly. Only an approved company workflow may change an assignment or commitment.
