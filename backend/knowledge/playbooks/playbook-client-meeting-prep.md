# Client meeting preparation

## Procedure status
Proposed; awaiting company approval. This procedure changes no database assignments or deadlines.

## Scope
Resolve client ID and the requested meeting date. If more than one client matches, ask the employee to choose.

## Evidence to collect
Read meetings and meeting_participants, recent communications, related issues and clients.account_manager_id. Distinguish recorded agenda/summary from suggested talking points.

## Proposed next action
List decisions to confirm, issue owners to contact and missing records. Do not invent commitments or meeting attendance.

## Stop conditions
Ask for clarification on competing entity matches. Report missing/unauthorized records without guessing. On dependency outage return an explicit service error rather than a fabricated successful result.
