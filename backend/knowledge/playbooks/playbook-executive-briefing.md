# Executive briefing generation

## Procedure status
Proposed; awaiting company approval. This procedure changes no database assignments or deadlines.

## Scope
Define the reporting window and scope; retain the viewer’s existing record permissions.

## Evidence to collect
Read delayed projects, unresolved urgent issues, client health, upcoming meetings and contract status/renewal dates. Do not infer revenue impact or summarize confidential financial fields.

## Proposed next action
Present source-backed observations, uncertainty and named recorded owners. Distinguish incomplete snapshots from company-wide totals.

## Stop conditions
Ask for clarification on competing entity matches. Report missing/unauthorized records without guessing. On dependency outage return an explicit service error rather than a fabricated successful result.
